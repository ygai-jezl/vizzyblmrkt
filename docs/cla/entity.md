# YouGrow Entity Contributor Licence Agreement v1

> Draft under legal review, not yet in force. Outside pull requests can't be merged until
> it is final (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)).

This agreement is between an organisation ("the Entity") and YouGrow.AI Limited ("we" or
"us"). It covers every Contribution submitted to the Project by the GitHub accounts the
Entity names in Schedule A, now and later. Its id is `entity-v1`.

Use it when an employer or another organisation has rights in the work its people make.
A person contributing only for themselves signs the [individual agreement](individual.md).

## 1. Definitions

- **Entity**: the organisation that signs this agreement, and every organisation that
  controls it, is controlled by it or is under common control with it. Control means
  owning more than half of the shares or votes, or the power to direct its management.
- **Named Accounts**: the GitHub accounts listed in Schedule A, as the Entity updates it
  under section 8.
- **Contribution**: any work of authorship, including a change to existing work, that a
  Named Account submits to us for the Project: a pull request, a commit, a patch, a
  suggestion in a review, or anything else sent for us to include.
- **Project**: the YouGrow software and documentation that we own or manage, in the
  YouGrow repository and anything published from it.
- **Submit**: to send to us or our representatives in any form, for example a pull
  request, a commit or a comment in our repository.

## 2. The Entity keeps its copyright

The Entity keeps the copyright in its Contributions. This agreement gives us a licence;
it doesn't transfer ownership.

## 3. Copyright licence

The Entity grants YouGrow.AI Limited, and those who receive software from us, a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to
reproduce, adapt, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,
sublicense and distribute its Contributions and any derivative works of them.

We may license the Contributions, alone or as part of the Project, under any licence
terms, open source or proprietary. This includes the GNU AGPL, the YouGrow Enterprise
Licence and commercial licences.

## 4. Patent licence

The Entity grants YouGrow.AI Limited, and those who receive software from us, a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent licence to make,
have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer its Contributions. It
covers only the patent claims the Entity can license that a Contribution infringes, alone
or combined with the Project as it was when the Contribution was submitted.

If anyone brings patent litigation (including a cross-claim or counterclaim) claiming that
a Contribution, or the Project, infringes a patent, every patent licence granted to them
under this agreement for that Contribution ends on the date the litigation is filed.

## 5. What the Entity confirms

- The Entity has the right to grant these licences for the work its Named Accounts
  submit, and the person signing has authority to sign for it.
- If any part of a Contribution is someone else's work, the Named Account says so when it
  submits it, with its source and its licence.
- The Entity is responsible for work its Named Accounts make with AI tools, as if they
  wrote it themselves. It confirms it has the right to submit that work under this
  agreement, and that, as far as it knows, the work doesn't copy anything it can't
  license on these terms.

## 6. Provided as is

Unless the law requires otherwise, or the Entity agrees to it in writing, the Entity
provides its Contributions as is, without warranties or conditions of any kind, including
warranties of title, non-infringement, merchantability or fitness for a particular
purpose. The Entity doesn't have to support its Contributions.

## 7. Tell us if anything changes

The Entity will tell us if it learns of anything that makes what it confirmed in
section 5 untrue.

## 8. Named Accounts

To add or remove a Named Account, a person with authority for the Entity emails the
contact below. Removing an account ends this agreement for that account's future
Contributions only; the licences for Contributions already submitted stay in place.

## How to sign

1. A person with authority for the Entity fills in Schedule A and "Signed for the Entity"
   (the Entity's legal name, their own name and role, and the date), signs, and sends the
   signed copy by email to the contact above.
2. A maintainer records the agreement in `docs/cla/policy.json`: an id for the Entity,
   `entity-v1`, the date it was signed, the Named Accounts' logins and who recorded it.
   No names or emails go in the repository.
3. The signed copy is kept privately, outside the repository.

From then on the CLA check accepts pull requests from the Named Accounts.
